Progression Necros / FM, 1995 — JavaScript port of PROG.EXE

0/0

Up/Down: song · Left/Right: previous/next order · Space: pause · Esc: stop · C: CRT scanlines

Pixels, fonts, texts and timings are rebuilt from the disassembly of PROG.EXE (360×480 unchained VGA, shown at 4:3 like a CRT). Sound: libopenmpt (the ModPlug engine) with cubic-spline interpolation instead of DSIK.
